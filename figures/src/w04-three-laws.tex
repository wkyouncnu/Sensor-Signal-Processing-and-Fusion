\parbox{206mm}{\raggedright\scriptsize
  \textbf{Why the injection point decides everything.} Inside the arctan, a term
  is \textbf{indistinguishable from more cross-track error} — ILOS steers as if
  the vessel were $2.25$\,m further out than it is. Outside it, a term is
  \textbf{indistinguishable from a rotated path} — ALOS steers along a line
  tilted by the drift it has estimated.\\[3pt]
  \textbf{And why the units are forced, not chosen.} A quantity added to $y_e^{\,p}$
  must be a length, which is why $\kappa y_{int}$ is in metres and $\kappa$
  carries the odd unit m/s (§4-8-3). A quantity added to an angle must be an
  angle, which is why $\hat\beta$ is simply in radians. The settled error falls
  from $2.253$\,m with no state to $0.008$\,m and $-0.004$\,m with one scalar
  each — roughly a factor of $300$.}
